# Example chat prompts

## Pain point 1 — after-meeting follow-up (what do I owe, what's out of date)

- `List every open commitment I've made to a customer and who's waiting on me, soonest first.`

## Pain point 2 — insights from customer meetings

- `What have customers told us they need from scheduled exports? Group it into must-haves versus nice-to-haves, with who said what.`
## Before-meeting prep (bonus)

- `I have a Nordkap check-in coming up. What's changed since we last spoke, what did I promise, and what are the open threads I should walk in ready to answer?`

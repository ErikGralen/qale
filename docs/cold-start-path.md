# The cold start, the path

Written 2026-09-06 after `docs/cold-start-map.md`. Five brainstorms ran in parallel on different
models and angles (the first ten minutes, the question ladder, the shape, the skeptical PM, the
machinery). This doc is the converged path. What each one argued and where they disagreed is at
the end. Nothing here is decided until the tickets in the next doc carry a Decision.

**Amended 2026-09-06 by `docs/cold-start-asking.md`:** give first, then ask. Every beat draws its facts as
a page before it asks anything, the ladder shrinks to five ask kinds with the epic bet and coverage
as statements, and there is one unowed-ask slot app-wide with a budget and quiet windows. Read the
two together.

**Amended again 2026-09-06 by `docs/cold-start-learning.md`:** the facts pass, the floors, the
question ladder and the getting-started session are dropped. In their place: one page Qale owns
("What I know"), free wonderings from the summary pass and every closing beat, and a second agent
fuelled by new material that tightens the page and asks at most one question. The Home card, the
opening copy changes, give-first, the budget and the funnel stand.

The goal, in Erik's words: minimise the time to value, and impress the PM early by asking the
right questions, so they think "this product gets it".

---

## The idea in one paragraph

Today the route is eight rows on a card, and every row is work the PM owes the app. The path
turns it into one conversation that the app owes the PM. The moment a source is readable (a
calendar, a Jira project, a dropped folder), Qale reads it, computes real facts about their
month with no model, and asks one question that could only come from having read. The answer
lands in memory. The next source arrives, the same conversation continues. Home shows the
question and the three things they can still do, nothing else. Week two is a daily count of
what the memory holds, a brief before each meeting once there is something to brief from, and a
weekly pass that reads what arrived against what the memory already says.

## The path, minute by minute

**0:00 to 2:30, the opening.** Six screens, same order. Three copy changes and no new screens:

- Hello says what the PM gets in three lines (meetings arrive on their own, drop what you have,
  it drafts and you approve) and one boundary line. The write policy paragraph folds.
- The key screen leads with "paste the key from your invite". The console route folds.
- Connections puts the calendar first with one trust line under each provider: read-only, the
  token stays in your Mac keychain, Qale has no server, it never writes to Jira without a card
  you press. The last two sentences exist nowhere on screen today and they are the product's
  strongest asset.

**2:30, Home.** Greeting, the two buttons, one card, the composer with its starters visible
from day one (they say what the product is for and hide exactly when that matters most). The
card is "Next": at most four rows, each a door, each with its own quiet "Not now". On day one:
add your key, connect your calendar, connect Jira or Confluence, add last month's meetings with
the six tool guides folded under it. The pitch card is deleted. Dismissing a row never removes
another.

**3:00, the calendar syncs.** Thirty days back land as meeting shells. The maintenance pass
computes the month's facts from the index with no model call: hours per person, repeating
series, external domains, meetings with no notes, and the rhythm `setup-pitch.ts` already
computes. It kicks the getting-started session with those facts and the session parks one
question. The top row of Next becomes the question itself, quoted, with Open beside it:

> Eleven hours last month with Elin Rask across six meetings, none with notes. Do you have last
> month's transcripts? Drop the folder and I read across all of them.

That is CM-1's backlog ask, moved from a card hint into a question that names their own
month. The tool guides ride along.

**5:00, they drop the folder.** Arrival files silently and counts on screen as today. When the
batch settles, the tick kicks the session again with the counts (filed, matched to meetings,
not filed). The session says what it now holds and asks the next question.

**7:00, the Jira ask, in the read's own words.** If Jira is not connected yet:

> Checkout Rewrite came up in four of your meetings. That work is in Jira, isn't it? Connect
> it and I read the tickets beside the meetings.

A token bought by evidence is a different transaction from a token bought by a promise. The
row opens Settings at the provider.

**9:00, the first read of Jira finishes.** Same kick, same session. Now the ladder can climb.
The first question is the cross-source one, and it is a bet, not a summary:

> Checkout Rewrite: 23 tickets moved last month and it came up in 6 of your 41 meetings. Is
> that what you get judged on this quarter?

Then, at most three questions per sitting, each built from a mechanical fact with a floor
under it, and skipped without comment when the floor is not met:

- "Five of your tickets say In Progress and have not moved in 24 days. Which are actually
  dead?" (rows unticked; a tick is a claim they never made)
- "Three people on your calendar are at bergmanfalk.se, five meetings this month. Customer,
  partner, or something else?"
- "In the Nordkap review on the 12th you said you would get the pricing model to Jonas by
  month end. Still yours?" (only after a transcript is filed, quoted verbatim)
- "Everything I have read is Checkout Rewrite, billing and the Bergman migration. Is anything
  big missing?" (shows its own blind spot)

Each answer writes: a confirmed claim into the understanding notes, verified by their yes and
still citing the source; a person or customer page as a proposal; a todo as its own card. The
seed card (tracked tickets and research pages, ticked, one confirm) closes the sitting.

**Day 2.** Next: the session has a question (or "3 proposals from yesterday's read"), today's
meeting has a brief ready, yesterday's meeting has nothing in it yet, add last month's
meetings. Footer, every day from now on: "Your memory holds 31 meetings, 9 with notes, 14
people, 6 tracked tickets, 2 decisions." That footer is how week 6 looks different from week 1
on the page everyone lands on.

**Day 9.** Next: "What changed this week, 4 proposals", Thursday's brief, one empty meeting.
The getting-started session retired after its third beat. Fresh sessions from here.

## What "this product gets it" is made of

Three rules, all of them copy in the skill file plus one mechanical pass:

1. **Every question carries a name and a number the PM can check.** The facts come off the
   index (calendar events, ticket motion, page edits, filed transcripts), never from a model.
   The model writes the sentence. A question with no source behind it is a form.
2. **Hypothesis first.** "Is that the main thing?" beats "tell me about your product". A no is
   better data than a yes, and a corrected hypothesis still writes.
3. **Floors and a cap.** An epic needs eight moved tickets, a stalled set three tickets at 14
   days, a person three meetings, a domain two people, a gap five note-less meetings. Under
   the floor the question is skipped and never mentioned. Three questions a sitting. If an
   answer produces nothing worth writing, the session stops and says what it now knows.

The one question every brainstorm would pick first is the stalled-tickets one. Jira knows the
dates and never asks. The PM knows the answer and never writes it down. It costs no model
spend, it is answered by tapping rows, and it is the first time the product tells them
something they did not want to hear.

## The shape underneath

**One getting-started session, re-entered by events.** The knock, the backlog debrief and the
interview are one conversation, not three sessions and a renderer scan. Events kick it: a
connection's first clean read (the calendar included; today that knock exists and is thrown
away), an arrival batch settling, the PM pressing a row or typing. Each kick is one user-role
message built from index counts at fire time, never from prose the model wrote.

No mailbox. The tick already retries anything it could not do. If the session has a turn open
or a question parked, the kick does nothing and the next tick re-reads the index and tries
again. The index is the queue. `done` is stamped after the fire returns an id, so a thrown
session is retried instead of lost.

The session retires on a context-free fact: three beats have run, or the memory holds more
than N authored pages. After that, events go to fresh sessions exactly as today. A session that
never retires is the main failure mode, and the retirement rule is the guard.

**Home is doors, not decisions.** The Next card draws the session's open question as its top
row, quoted, and the question is answered in the session. Proposals group by session ("3
proposals from Friday's read"), never one row per card. Hard cap of four rows, no "see all",
nothing decided on Home. That is what keeps it from drifting back into the Inbox that was
deleted on 2026-09-05.

**The mechanical facts pass.** One function over the index that computes every question's
evidence and its floor. It absorbs `setup-pitch.ts` (rhythm and series) and adds: hours per
attendee, external domains, note-less meetings, tickets assigned to the PM with no motion,
epics with motion joined by name to meeting titles, things assigned to the PM by others. It is
arithmetic, so per Erik's rule it may be a gate. It is also the biggest build in phase one.

**`grouped` stays deferred, and the copy stops promising it.** The checked-row ask already
covers the cold-start moments: a new page confirmed on a ticked list is `asked: true` and lands
silently with a commit and a revert. Rewrites keep their cards. The Settings section and
PRODUCT.md drop the sentence about one card per intent until the card exists. It comes back
with the consolidation pass, which cannot ship without it.

**Meeting-prep gets a gate.** No brief when the memory holds nothing about the meeting's people
or title. A brief from an empty memory is a horoscope, and it is the first unasked thing the PM
sees.

## Week two

Two things, in this order.

**The daily tally.** The Next card's footer, from the index, every day: authored pages with a
body, meetings with notes, people, tracked tickets, decisions, verified claims. Labels and
mirrors never count. It costs nothing and it is always true.

**The consolidation pass (M5), with `grouped`.** Not the coach, which asks for work in weekly
form and changes nothing in the memory, and not the watched folder yet. M5 is the only engine
that changes what the memory says: two insights that are one, a customer page that stopped
being true, a decision nobody repointed. Trigger on the tick when three or more sources were
processed since the last pass, at most weekly, otherwise end quietly at no cost. Output: one
session, "What changed this week", grouped proposals with a citation on every line. That
session is the day-9 row. It needs `grouped`, so they are one build.

The watched folder (CM-8) revives on evidence: if `source.added` stays at zero for a workspace
after three weeks, that is the number the decision was waiting for.

## Measurement

Five numbers, sent as bands, consent screen updated:

1. Key in the first sitting (exists: `onboarding.step` plus `hasKey` on launch).
2. Time to first content: new `spine.kicked` with the event kind and an age-since-install band.
3. The first question answered or dismissed, and how long it waited: new `ask.decided` with
   skill and offered flag. This is the "does it get it" number.
4. First proposal decided: a `first` flag on `card.decided`.
5. Memory size on day 1 and day 7 of the same install: new `memory.measured` once a day at the
   first tick, bands only. Also "calendar attempted, abandoned in the browser", because if that
   number is large the calendar-first order is wrong and only the number will say so.

## Spend

Day one is the heavy day: one read turn over the site, five to ten interview turns on the
interactive model, a backlog of 20 transcripts as 20 Sonnet skims plus five full reads, and up
to a hundred Haiku label calls. Days two to seven: two to four session turns as batches land,
labels near zero, the librarian at most twice a day and never on a workspace with no findings.
A steady week: the spine is retired and costs nothing, the daily tally is arithmetic, M5 is one
Sonnet run a week when there is fuel. The coach as an agent is never worth a model call.

## Build, in phases

Sizes are honest guesses.

**Phase 1a, the spine (about four days).**

1. The getting-started skill file, seeded like the others: a calendar beat (name the month,
   ask for the folder, name the tools), a first-read beat, a batch beat, retirement. `tell-qale`
   keeps the interview and loses its First look section. One day, mostly words.
2. `kickSpine(event)` in handlers, called from the first-read path, the arrival settle and the
   Next row; the session id in settings; idle check before firing; `done` after the fire. One day.
3. The Next card replacing both cards: four ranked rows from `attention.ts` interleaved with the
   setup rows, per-row Not now in the check ledger, proposals grouped by session. Two days.
4. Delete `SetupPitch`, the pitch localStorage key, `onboarding.dismissed`, the four non-setup
   First steps rows and their stamps (keep `understanding`, the `told` branch reads it), the
   FD-5 double door. Half a day.
5. Starters visible from day one. Copy: Hello, key, connection trust lines, the `grouped`
   sentence out of Settings and PRODUCT.md. Half a day.

**Phase 1b, the questions (about five days).**

6. The facts pass over the index with floors, absorbing `setup-pitch.ts`. Two to three days.
7. The question ladder as skill copy: hypothesis first, evidence per row, "you are reading
   that wrong" as a first-class answer, three a sitting, stop when nothing writes. One day.
8. Telemetry: the four new events and the consent lines. Two days.

**Phase 2, quiet value (about two days).**

9. The daily tally footer from the index. Half a day.
10. The meeting-prep gate. Half a day.
11. Retirement rule and the N constant. Half a day.

**Phase 3, week two (about two weeks, after a week of funnel data).**

12. `grouped`: group id on proposals, per-row staleness, partial accept, per-row Activity, the
    card. A week.
13. M5: a consolidation skill file, a selector over the processing ledger, the weekly floor,
    grouped cards. A week.

## How it fails, and the guards

- **The spine becomes a wall.** Every event appends, the transcript outgrows the window, the
  model re-asks day two's question. Guards: kicks carry counts, one open question at a time,
  retirement on the third beat.
- **The facts are right and the reading is wrong.** A ticket parked on purpose, an epic that is
  a dump. Guards: evidence inline on every row, a correction is an answer not a failure, and a
  corrected hypothesis still writes.
- **The calendar give dead-ends in OAuth.** An unverified Google app screen or a locked-down
  Workspace admin turns the first give into the first wall. Guards: the backlog drop is a peer
  on the same screen, never a fallback; the abandoned-in-browser event is built first; and the
  Google Cloud client has to be a verified one before beta, which is a task outside this doc.
- **No fuel.** A PM who never drops a transcript and connects nothing gets a memory of ticket
  mirrors and empty shells, and M5 idles. Guards: the calendar beat asks for the folder by
  name, the capture rows on Next name the meetings, and the funnel measures both so the watched
  folder revives on evidence rather than on a hunch.
- **The Next card becomes the Inbox.** Guard: four rows, every row a door, nothing decided on
  Home.

## What the five argued, and what was dropped

All five agreed on: delete the pitch card, one card slot on Home with per-row dismissal, the
knock must land as a visible row, the debrief asks for the backlog itself, questions carry
names and numbers from the index, stamp `done` after the fire, build the funnel.

They disagreed on three things.

**One long-lived session or a ranked card.** The shape brainstorm rejected the session
(settings are not decisions, a two-week transcript is a wall). The machinery brainstorm found
that `run()` on an existing id already appends a turn, and that a parked ask already survives a
quit. Both are right, so the path takes both: the session carries the conversation, the card
carries the doors, and the settings rows never enter the session.

**Build `grouped` now or not.** Two said now, because the seed card and M5 need it. The
machinery brainstorm sized it at a week (group id, staleness per row, partial accept, per-row
Activity, a card) and showed the checked-row ask covers the cold-start moments. Deferred to
phase 3 with M5, and the copy that promises it comes out now.

**Reorder the opening.** The skeptic put the calendar before the key and Jira after a give.
The path keeps six screens in today's order and gets the same effect by moving the Jira ask
into the conversation, where the read makes it in its own words, and by putting the trust
lines on the connection rows. Merging the key and connections into one screen (the first-ten-
minutes brainstorm) was dropped: the key screen is where the clarity review found the biggest
gap, and burying it in a row does not close that.

Dropped outright: a "Waiting for you" slot holding one question and nothing else (the Next card
does that and three more things), the coach as a model-driven agent (it is a count), and a
persistent getting-started page or tour.

## Decisions this doc needs before tickets

1. The session shape: one re-entered getting-started session, retiring on the third beat.
2. The Next card: four rows, doors only, per-row Not now, daily tally footer. The pitch card
   and the eight-row checklist go.
3. `grouped` deferred to phase 3 with M5, and the copy that promises it comes out now.
4. The question ladder's floors and the three-a-sitting cap live in skill copy, the facts pass
   is a gate.
5. Phase order: 1a, 1b, 2, then a week of funnel data before phase 3.
6. Whether the Google Cloud client gets verified before beta, since calendar-first depends on it.

# Brasserie Lund

Restaurant chain, 40 restaurants across southern Sweden. Our largest account and the one every
roadmap argument eventually mentions. Renewal is 2026-12-01. Head of operations is
[[people/lena-strand]]; [[people/marcus-ek]] owns the relationship and
[[people/ulrika-nystrom]] is customer success.

Their year has a shape. Christmas bookings open on 1 November, and December is when guests
book a table for twelve and six turn up. Last year December no-shows cost them about one table a
night per restaurant ([[insights/brasserie-lund-december-no-shows]]). What they do about it
today is a phone call the afternoon before, by hand, at every restaurant
([[notes/christmas-season-playbook]]).

# Bruno's Burgers

Burger chain, 35 restaurants, mostly student and part-time staff on short shifts. Steady
account, pays on time, low support volume. CS manager is [[people/ulrika-nystrom]]; the manager
she brings to most calls is [[people/petra-alm]] in Gothenburg.

They are heavy schedulers — high staff churn, lots of small shifts, managers building the week
themselves rather than in a central ops team. On paper they look like the account that should
have the most to say about how the schedule is built, and they say almost nothing to us.

## Commitments

- None open.

## Watch

  place to fix that.
- Nothing on the H2 plan was scoped with them in mind ([[themes/shift-swaps]],

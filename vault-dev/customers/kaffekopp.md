# Kaffekopp

Small café chain with one central office. Churned at the end of November 2025.

The story is short and it is ours. In 2025 they asked twice for text reminders: guests booked a
table for Sunday brunch and did not turn up, and a text the day before was the thing they
wanted. We built it. Reminders shipped, one text per booking, the day before at 17:00. Nobody
told them. By November they had moved to a competitor that "had reminders".

Nothing was broken. The feature existed, worked, and was on a changelog nobody outside this
building reads. What was missing was the list of accounts that had asked, and one person whose
job it was to call them.

Their ops lead said the thing that still gets repeated here: "we asked, you said you'd look at
it, and then we stopped hearing from you."

## Why it stays in the vault

Because it is the cheapest lesson we own, and it keeps applying. A request that arrives through
support and never becomes a note is a request that cannot be answered when the answer finally
exists. Today the request is the double reminder, and the accounts waiting are
[[customers/sjogatan]] and [[customers/pizzeria-napoli]] ([[people/ulrika-nystrom]],
[[people/petra-alm]], [[people/jonas-berg]]).

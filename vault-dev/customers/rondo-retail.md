# Rondo Retail

Fashion retail chain in Gothenburg, 60 seats. Churned at contract end, 2026-06-30.

Johanna did the exit interview on 2026-07-10. The main reason was the weekly ops report: their
coordinator rebuilt it by hand every Monday, and when she resigned nobody wanted to inherit the
job. Price was the second reason and mostly served as the argument they took to their CFO
([[insights/rondo-churned-over-reporting-gaps]], [[meetings/2026-07-13-cs-sync]]).

## Win-back
Johanna has kept the relationship warm and thinks she can get a second meeting once scheduled
exports actually ship ([[decisions/2026-07-08-pilot-scheduled-exports]]). We'd be going back
with a fix for the exact thing that lost the account.

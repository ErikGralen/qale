Two prospects asked whether Rota works without a connection, so I put it to Åsa. The answer was
no, and the reasoning is worth keeping. Managers build schedules in a back office with wifi.
Staff read their shifts on a phone, and the app already caches the current and next week, so the
one thing people actually do on a train works today.

What offline would really buy is offline *editing*, and that means a sync engine with conflict
resolution on an object two managers edit at once. Rebecca's rough read was most of a quarter of
Scheduling's time, against zero customers who have asked in writing.

Åsa's words: "we are not doing offline mode." I'd rather spend the quarter on
[[themes/shift-swaps]] and [[themes/payroll-export]], which chains ask for by name.

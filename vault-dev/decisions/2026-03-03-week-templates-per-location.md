Week templates shipped in August 2025 scoped to one location, and I decided to keep them there.
The argument was that no two locations staff the same way: a city café opens at seven with three
people, a mall unit opens at ten with one, and a template that pretends otherwise gets edited
into something worse than an empty week.

There was a second reason I liked less at the time and like less now. Sharing a template
chain-wide needs role names to mean the same thing in every location, and ours are free text.

I was wrong about how much that mattered. Café Nord's regional managers were rebuilding the same
template by hand across dozens of locations, which is exactly the work templates exist to remove.

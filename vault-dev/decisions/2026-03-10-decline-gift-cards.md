Sales brought gift cards back from a prospect's requirements list, so I put it to Åsa a second
time. The answer was no, and the reasoning is worth keeping. A gift card is a payments product:
a balance the restaurant owes the guest, refunds, accounting, and a card that has to work at the
till as well as online. Bord is a bookings product. Nothing we have built helps with any of that.

What the ask usually means, once unpicked, is "can a guest pay for a table in advance". That is
deposits, which the Payments team owns ([[tickets/jira/PAY-210]]), and it is the right answer to
give sales.

Åsa's words: "we are not doing gift cards." Roadmap H2 lists it under "Not doing" so nobody has
to ask again ([[wikipages/confluence/roadmap-h2]]).

One auth migration at a time. SSO unblocks security reviews and those are pass/fail; SCIM
removes ongoing pain, which is bad but survivable. So SSO ships first, SCIM scoping starts in
August for September delivery.

Nordkap accepted the sequencing at the QBR, on the condition that SSO doesn't slip. Sara
reconfirmed on 2026-07-14 that September works, while making a point of mentioning another 14
accounts her team had created by hand that week.

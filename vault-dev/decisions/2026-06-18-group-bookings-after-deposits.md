Group bookings ([[tickets/jira/BOK-520]]) does not start until the Payments team has shipped
deposits ([[tickets/jira/PAY-210]]): charge at booking, refund on cancellation. Payments plans
that for Q4, so group bookings is Q1 2027.

Åsa's reason: a set menu with no deposit is a bigger no-show, not a smaller one. A party of
twelve that books a set menu and does not turn up costs the restaurant the food as well as the
table. Shipping the booking without the deposit would build the thing chains complain about
most.

Marcus asked for it sooner, for a prospect, and was told no. The answer to sales is the
quarter, Q1 2027, and the reason. Not "on the roadmap".

The epic was created the same day with a Blocks link from PAY-210. Roadmap H2 says the same
under "Next" ([[wikipages/confluence/roadmap-h2]]).

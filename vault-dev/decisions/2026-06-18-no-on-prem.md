We will not offer customer-managed installs. An engineering team of nine can't support versioned
on-prem deployments without it consuming the roadmap, and the asks so far unpack into data
control and auditability rather than hardware ([[insights/on-prem-asks-are-security-asks]]).

Standard counter-offer: EU-only region, audit log, ISO 27001, DPA. Fenno Energi got exactly that
package; their security board has it now.

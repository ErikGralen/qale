We will not offer customer-managed installs. Nine engineers cannot support versioned on-prem
deployments without it taking over the roadmap, and the asks we've had so far turn out to be
about data control and auditability rather than hardware
([[insights/on-prem-asks-are-security-asks]]).

The standard counter-offer is the EU-only region, the audit log, ISO 27001 and the DPA. Fenno
Energi got exactly that package and their security board has it now.

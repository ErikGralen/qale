Brasserie Lund opens Christmas bookings on 1 November, and December is the month that pays for
the year. It is also the month with the most no-shows: a table of twelve booked weeks ahead,
and six turn up. Lena put a number on it at the review on 2026-06-02: about one
table a night per restaurant last year, across 40 restaurants.

What they do about it today is a phone call the afternoon before, by hand, at every restaurant.
It catches some. It does not catch the guest who says yes on the phone and does not come.

This is the strongest pull we have for [[research/no-show-fees]], and it has a date attached
that we did not choose. 1 November is not a preference; it is when their Christmas bookings
open, and a fee that is not live by then does nothing for this December. Their renewal is
2026-12-01, in the middle of the month the fee is for.

High confidence: 40 restaurants, the same story every December, told by the person who owns it.

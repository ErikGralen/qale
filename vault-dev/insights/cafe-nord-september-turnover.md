Roughly a third of Café Nord's café staff are students, and they change over between mid-August
and late September when terms start. Lena described the result at the QBR: for two or three weeks
her managers rebuild schedules by hand, and the staff who are still there trade shifts between
themselves over text messages that never reach Rota. The schedule in the system stops matching
who is actually working, which is the part that costs her, because payroll and the schedule then
disagree.

This is the strongest pull we have for [[research/shift-swaps]], and it has a date attached that we
did not choose. September is not a preference, it is when their year starts. Their renewal is
2026-12-01, three months after the pain peaks.

High confidence: 120 locations, the same story every autumn, told by the person who owns it.

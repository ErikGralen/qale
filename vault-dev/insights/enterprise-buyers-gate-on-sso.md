Every enterprise evaluation since autumn 2025 has had SSO on the pass/fail list. Two of the
deals we lost last year named it in the debrief. Sara put Nordkap's review in plain terms at
the QBR: "It's a checkbox and right now you fail it." When David asked Elin at Bergman & Falk
what would be an automatic no, she answered "No SSO would be."

Above roughly 200 seats we should assume login through the customer's own IdP is a
precondition for anyone even looking at the product.

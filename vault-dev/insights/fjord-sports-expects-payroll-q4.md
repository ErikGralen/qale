Oskar is not waiting to hear whether we will do payroll export. He is scheduling around the date
I gave him. Fjord Sports move 60 stores onto Visma over the autumn, and the plan he described has
approved hours flowing out of Rota before the cutover, so store managers stop keying spreadsheets
into two systems.

That makes our Q4 both a commitment and a dependency in someone else's project plan. If the Visma
connector slips, they do not simply wait; they either delay a migration that has other moving
parts in it or they build the manual bridge they were trying to remove.

Two things make this fragile on our side. Visma is the second connector by design
([[decisions/2026-05-20-fortnox-first-payroll-target]]), and it cannot start before
[[tickets/jira/PLT-77]] lands. Neither is Oskar's problem, and neither is a reason he would
accept for a date moving. He asked whether Q4 means October or December and I have not answered.

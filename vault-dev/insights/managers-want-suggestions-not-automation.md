summary: Managers want a suggested schedule they can edit, not one the system publishes for them

Four manager interviews, and the same instinct in all four: a suggestion is welcome, an automatic
schedule is not. Nobody objected to the system proposing next week from last week plus
availability. Everybody objected to it being the schedule until they had looked at it.

The reasons were not about trust in the algorithm. They were about knowledge the system does not
have. One manager is holding Thursday evenings for someone who is studying for exams. Another
knows two people cannot work the same close. A third puts her strongest person on the day the
delivery arrives. None of that is in Rota and none of them wanted to type it in.

So the shape to build, if we build it, is a draft the manager edits and publishes, with the
changes visible against what was suggested. Medium confidence: four interviews is thin, and I ran
them, which means I probably asked leading questions at least twice.

The people who need the Monday numbers are depot managers and finance, and they don't open
dashboards. So someone on the ops team turns the dashboards into a PDF by hand and emails it
round.

Mikkel on his Monday: "Lise spends an hour, sometimes more, screenshotting dashboards". Rondo
ran the same standing job until the person doing it resigned and nobody would take it over.
Johanna's exit call named it the same way: "Their ops coordinator rebuilt it by hand every
Monday."

Format matters more than I expected. Managers want a PDF they can print and forward. Finance
wants CSV of the same numbers. If we can only ship one first it's the PDF.

Both times self-hosting has come up, asking "what is the policy protecting against?" surfaced
the same three things: where data lives, who can access it, and whether that's auditable. The
EU-only region, the audit log and ISO 27001 answered most of the concern in the room — Antti
moved from "policy says own iron" to taking our package to his security board. Sales should
probe the underlying concern before treating an on-prem ask as disqualifying.

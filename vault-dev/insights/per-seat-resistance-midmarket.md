Kranelund take on around 40 seasonal drivers every summer, and Mikkel won't pay a full seat
for someone who opens one screen for three months: "I can't defend that to my CFO." He's
raised it twice now and says it will come up at renewal whether he brings it or we do.

That's the only account about seasonal headcount. Two others lean on the model for their own
reasons. Price was the second reason Rondo gave for leaving, and Johanna's read of that was:
"Second, and mostly as ammunition." Nordkap procurement has now opened a review, which Sara
says will arrive as questions about "the per-seat model, usage stats, seat activity, the usual
squeeze." Could be one complaint or three unrelated ones. Worth another quarter of watching
before anyone touches the model.

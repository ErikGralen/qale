From Johanna's exit interview. Rondo's ops coordinator rebuilt the weekly report by hand every
Monday. She resigned in May, nobody would take the job over, and somebody in the room asked why
they were paying for a tool that needed a person attached to it. The renewal died there.

Price came up second. Johanna reads it as cover: "if the report had been automated I think
they'd have swallowed the price." The CFO needed a reason that sounded strategic.

Worth a win-back attempt once scheduled exports ship
([[decisions/2026-07-08-pilot-scheduled-exports]]). Johanna has kept the relationship warm.

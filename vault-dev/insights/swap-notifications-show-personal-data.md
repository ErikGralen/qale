When a staff member proposes a swap, the colleague on the other side gets a notification carrying
the requester's name and phone number. It was built that way for a sensible reason — the two of
them need to be able to talk — and it went in without anyone asking whether we are allowed to do
it. Rebecca noticed and flagged it in our 1:1.

The staff whose numbers we would be sharing are not our customers. They are our customers'
employees, they never agreed to anything with us, and a phone number pushed to a colleague is a
disclosure whether or not anyone minds in practice. Some chains will mind on their staff's
behalf.

Medium confidence because the legal read is not in: [[people/henrik-dahl]] has not looked at it
yet ([[todos/henrik-review-swap-notifications]]). The fact itself is certain. What is uncertain
is whether the fix is consent, in-app messaging without the number, or nothing at all. Better
found now than in a release note.

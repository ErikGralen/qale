## Summary

Oskar walked us through the Visma migration. Fjord Sports move their 60 stores onto Visma over
the autumn and want approved hours coming out of Rota rather than being keyed in by store
managers, which is what happens today for their seasonal staff. I told him the payroll export
lands in Q4: CSV and Fortnox first in Q3, the Visma connector after that. He took the date and
said he would plan the cutover for late Q4 on the back of it.

## Summary

Lena spent most of the hour on September. Café Nord's student staff turn over at the start of the
academic year, and for two or three weeks her managers rebuild schedules by hand while people
trade shifts over text messages that never reach Rota. She asked, directly, when staff will be
able to swap shifts in the app.

Marcus answered before I could: "we'll have swaps for you before September." I said in the same
breath that we are targeting Q4, that swaps sit behind payroll export in the H2 order
([[decisions/2026-05-18-h2-order-payroll-first]]), and that I would not want her planning around
September. Lena wrote down September. That is the number she left with, and Marcus did not walk
it back.

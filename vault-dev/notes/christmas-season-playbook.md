# Christmas season playbook

What the chains do from November to the end of December, and what we tell them. Written for
the CSMs, so every account gets the same advice.

## What the season looks like

- Christmas bookings open on 1 November for most chains. Brasserie Lund opens on that day every
  year.
- The big evenings are the Fridays and Saturdays from the last week of November to the week
  before Christmas. Most restaurants are full by mid-November.
- Company parties book early and book big. Many book two or three restaurants and keep the one
  they like.

## Why no-shows peak

- A guest who booked in early November has forgotten by mid-December. A reminder helps, but a
  text does not stop a party of twelve from choosing another place.
- Company parties are booked by one person for many. If the plan changes, nobody cancels.
- A full evening has no walk-in cover. An empty table at 19:00 stays empty.

Brasserie Lund's number: about one no-show table a night per restaurant last December, over 40
restaurants ([[insights/brasserie-lund-december-no-shows]]).

## What Bord recommends today

1. Reminders on for every restaurant. One text, the day before at 17:00.
2. For parties over eight, ring the guest a week before. The booking list shows party size, so
   a manager can filter for them.
3. Close online bookings for an evening once it is full. A guest who cannot book online rings,
   and a phone booking is one a manager has talked to.

## What is coming

No-show fees ([[tickets/jira/BOK-300]]): a card at booking, charged if the guest does not turn
up. Roadmap H2 says Q4 ([[wikipages/confluence/roadmap-h2]]). Do not give a month to a
customer until the stories are estimated.

# Competitors

## Insikt (Stockholm)

Report-first pitch: "your Monday numbers in your inbox, no dashboards to babysit" — squarely at our scheduled-reporting gap. Workspace pricing rather than per-seat, which lands well with seasonal-headcount accounts. Weak where we're strong: no SSO/SCIM, no audit log, no certifications — they can't pass an enterprise security review today. The unconfirmed Kranelund demo ([[insights/kranelund-evaluating-insikt]]) would be exactly their profile of account.

## Corvid Analytics (Boston)

Full enterprise suite: SSO, SCIM, SOC 2, the works. Wins on compliance checklists, loses on price and deployment time — their implementations run quarters, ours run weeks. When we lose enterprise deals on missing checkboxes, this is who wins them. SSO + SCIM shipping this quarter closes most of that gap for Nordic mid-enterprise.

We lose down-market on price and delivery format (Insikt), up-market on checkbox completeness (Corvid). Q3 plan attacks both ends: exports v1 and enterprise auth.

# Competitors

## Insikt (Stockholm)

Report-first pitch: "your Monday numbers in your inbox, no dashboards to babysit". That's aimed straight at our scheduled-reporting gap. They price per workspace rather than per seat, which goes down well with anyone carrying seasonal headcount. Where they're thin: no SSO, no SCIM, no audit log, no certifications. They can't get through an enterprise security review today. The unconfirmed Kranelund demo ([[insights/kranelund-evaluating-insikt]]) is exactly the kind of account they'd win.

## Corvid Analytics (Boston)

Full enterprise suite. SSO, SCIM, SOC 2, all of it. They win on compliance checklists and lose on price and time to value; their implementations run in quarters where ours run in weeks. When we lose an enterprise deal over a missing checkbox, this is usually who takes it. SSO and SCIM shipping this quarter closes most of that gap for Nordic mid-enterprise.

Short version: we lose down-market on price and delivery format, and up-market on checkbox completeness. The Q3 plan goes after both ends at once, exports v1 and enterprise auth.

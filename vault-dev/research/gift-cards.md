# Gift cards

Stance: won't do. Decided in [[decisions/2026-03-10-decline-gift-cards]].

The ask is a gift card a guest buys on the restaurant's booking page and spends on a visit.
Declined twice, the second time on 2026-03-10. The reasoning has not moved: a gift card is a
payments product, with balances, refunds and accounting on the restaurant's side, and Bord is a
bookings product.

It comes back through sales rather than through restaurants, usually as a line on a prospect's
requirements list rather than a described problem. When we have unpicked it, the real question
has been "can a guest pay for a table in advance", which is the deposits work on the Payments
team ([[tickets/jira/PAY-210]]), not a gift card.

This page exists so the next ask is cheap to answer. Roadmap H2 lists it under "Not doing"
([[wikipages/confluence/roadmap-h2]]). If a chain ever loses a renewal over it, reopen with
that account named.

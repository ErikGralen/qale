# Group bookings

Stance: exploring, Q1 2027.

A party over eight books a set menu and pays a deposit at booking. Today a group booking is a
phone call, an email with the menu, and a bank transfer the restaurant chases by hand. The
no-show on a table of twelve with no deposit is the most expensive kind.

Exploring. Epic is [[tickets/jira/BOK-520]], To Do, nothing estimated. It waits on the Payments
team's deposits work, [[tickets/jira/PAY-210]]: charge at booking, refund on cancellation.
Payments plans that for Q4 and Henrik has given no date
([[meetings/2026-07-09-steering]], [[todos/ask-henrik-for-pay-210-date]]). Åsa decided on
2026-06-18 that group bookings follows deposits, so Q1 2027
([[decisions/2026-06-18-group-bookings-after-deposits]]). Roadmap H2 says the same
([[wikipages/confluence/roadmap-h2]]).

Who asks: [[customers/nordic-steak]], a prospect with 25 restaurants, through
[[people/marcus-ek]]. Oskar wants it written into the contract
([[insights/nordic-steak-wants-group-bookings-in-contract]]). Marcus has asked twice to move
it up and was told no both times.

The sentence to keep saying to sales: a set menu with no deposit is a bigger no-show, not a
smaller one. Q1 2027 holds until Payments names a date.

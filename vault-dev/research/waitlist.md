# Waitlist

Stance: committed, Q4 after no-show fees.

When an evening is full, a guest joins the waitlist from the booking page. When a table frees,
because a booking is cancelled or marked as a no-show, the first guest on the list gets a text
and a link to take it. Today that guest phones the restaurant, and the restaurant keeps a paper
list by the till.

Committed and second in the H2 order ([[decisions/2026-05-14-h2-order-no-show-fees-first]]).
Epic is [[tickets/jira/BOK-260]]. Joining the list from the booking page is done
([[tickets/jira/BOK-262]], 2026-07-10). Texting the first guest when a table frees is in
progress ([[tickets/jira/BOK-265]]).

The two epics fit together: a no-show fee frees the table earlier, and the waitlist fills it.
That is why the order is fees first. Both sit on Rebecca's team, so they trade a quarter with
each other ([[meetings/2026-07-13-1-1-rebecca]]).

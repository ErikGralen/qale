# Guide: discovery

Load this when synthesizing an interview or a customer meeting and the notes lean on solutions or
feature requests rather than the problem underneath.

- Anchor on the last real occurrence, not hypotheticals: "when did this last bite you?"
- Separate the symptom ("the export is slow") from the job ("I reconcile invoices every Friday").
- Quantify the pain: frequency, time lost, money at risk, who else is blocked.
- A requested feature is a hypothesis, not a requirement. Record the problem it claims to solve.
- One loud account is not a pattern until a second account cites the same problem.

When you need the actual wording for a live interview, read
`skills/discovery-guide/question-bank.md`: the questions grouped by what they are trying to learn,
plus the ones not to ask. Read only the group you need.

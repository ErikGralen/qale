# Auto-schedule suggestions

Rota already knows last week's schedule, who is available, contracted hours and roles. The idea
is to open next week half-built: a draft the manager corrects rather than an empty grid they
fill in.

Exploring. No epic, no estimate, nothing promised to anybody. What we have is four manager
interviews ([[notes/auto-schedule-interviews]]) and one finding that keeps repeating: they want
a suggestion they can edit, not a schedule that publishes itself
([[insights/managers-want-suggestions-not-automation]]). The word "automatic" made every one of
them list the things we would get wrong — who is off sick, who is on their way out, which two
people can't work the same shift.

The strongest case for it is the September rebuild at [[customers/cafe-nord]]
([[insights/cafe-nord-september-turnover]]), where the hand-work is measured in weeks. It is
also the reason to be careful: a bad suggestion during that exact week costs more trust than a
blank grid.

Not competing for H2 capacity. Revisit when swaps have shipped and we know what managers
actually approve.

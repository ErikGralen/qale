# Mobile access

This comes up sideways rather than as a demand. A Kranelund driver asked whether there's an
app, and support sees a trickle of tickets about dashboards on phones. Nobody has made it a
condition of buying or renewing.

Held out of Q3 ([[decisions/2026-07-08-hold-mobile-until-q4]]). Before Q4 planning, someone
needs to pull the actual mobile-web usage numbers and count the support tickets, so we're
arguing about a number instead of two anecdotes.

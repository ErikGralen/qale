# Internal voice

Internal updates are three labelled lines: Shipped, Changed, Watch. Set on 2026-07-10. Change this line and the next update follows it.

### Three labelled lines
Shipped, Changed, Watch. One line each, with a date or a number on every line.
Ticket keys go in brackets at the end of a line, never inside the sentence. No greeting, nothing else.

## How it sounds

The reader is a colleague in leadership, CS, sales or support who reads `#product-updates` on a phone between two meetings.

- The fact first, then the date. "Table areas shipped 2026-07-14", not "we are pleased to say".
- A date or a number on every line. "Four support tickets", not "several".
- Say it flat. "No-show fees: end of October", not "we are hoping for October".
- Say what changed for restaurants in plain words, and put the key in brackets at the end of the line: "(BOK-412)".
- Plain words over trade words: "we stopped work on X", not "we deprioritised X".
- No greeting, no sign-off, no "hope you are well".
- Never write: "just wanted to", "circle back", "synergy", "leverage" as a verb, "touch base".
